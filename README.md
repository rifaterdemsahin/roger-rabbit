🎭 **Roger Rabbit** is the title character of the 1988 live-action/animated film Who Framed Roger Rabbit, directed by Robert Zemeckis and produced by Steven Spielberg.

🌆 The film is set in a world where cartoon characters ("Toons") live alongside humans. Roger Rabbit, an energetic and accident-prone cartoon rabbit, is framed for the murder of a wealthy businessman. A private detective, Eddie Valiant, reluctantly takes the case and uncovers a much larger conspiracy.

✨ Some notable aspects of the film:

* 🖌️ It was groundbreaking for seamlessly combining live-action actors with hand-drawn animation.
* 🐭🐰🦆 It featured characters from multiple studios—including Mickey Mouse, Bugs Bunny, Donald Duck, and Daffy Duck—appearing together, something that was extremely rare.
* 💃 Roger's glamorous wife, Jessica Rabbit, became one of the most iconic animated characters in film history. Her famous line is: *"I'm not bad, I'm just drawn that way."*

🏆 The movie won four Academy Awards (including a Special Achievement Award for animation) and is widely regarded as one of the greatest films blending animation with live action.
