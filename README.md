# 🐰 roger-rabbit

**Animation in the real world** — an experiment in bringing cartoon-style animation back into live-action footage using AI video tools.

🔗 **Live site:** https://rifaterdemsahin.github.io/roger-rabbit/
▶️ **Reference clip:** https://youtu.be/RWtt3Tmnij4

---

## 🎯 The Goal

*Who Framed Roger Rabbit* (1988) proved that hand-drawn "Toons" could convincingly share the screen with live actors and the real world. This project asks whether today's AI video tools can recreate that same magic.

The plan is to use **[Google Flow](https://labs.google/flow)**, Google's AI filmmaking tool, to take an ordinary live-action clip and reimagine it with the energy of classic cartoon animation — a modern, AI-assisted take on the toon-meets-reality trick that made the original film groundbreaking. The minimalist, single-continuous-line style of **[La Linea](La_Linea.md)** is a second reference point for how far animation can be pushed with almost nothing but a line.

👉 See the **[live demo page](https://rifaterdemsahin.github.io/roger-rabbit/)** for the first Google Flow example and a walkthrough of the goal.

## 🎭 Background: Who Framed Roger Rabbit

**Roger Rabbit** is the title character of the 1988 live-action/animated film *Who Framed Roger Rabbit*, directed by Robert Zemeckis and produced by Steven Spielberg.

The film is set in a world where cartoon characters ("Toons") live alongside humans. Roger Rabbit, an energetic and accident-prone cartoon rabbit, is framed for the murder of a wealthy businessman. A private detective, Eddie Valiant, reluctantly takes the case and uncovers a much larger conspiracy.

✨ Some notable aspects of the film:

* 🖌️ It was groundbreaking for seamlessly combining live-action actors with hand-drawn animation.
* 🐭🐰🦆 It featured characters from multiple studios — including Mickey Mouse, Bugs Bunny, Donald Duck, and Daffy Duck — appearing together, something that was extremely rare.
* 💃 Roger's glamorous wife, Jessica Rabbit, became one of the most iconic animated characters in film history. Her famous line is: *"I'm not bad, I'm just drawn that way."*

🏆 The movie won four Academy Awards (including a Special Achievement Award for animation) and is widely regarded as one of the greatest films blending animation with live action.
